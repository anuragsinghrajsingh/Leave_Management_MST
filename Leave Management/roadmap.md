# Future Roadmap: Employee Management Enhancements

This document captures high-value features planned for future implementation to further enhance the Employee ID and profile systems.

## 🚀 Upcoming "WOW" Features

### 1. Digital ID Card Generator (PDF)
- **Description:** A tool to generate beautifully designed, branded company ID cards.
- **Components:** Includes employee photo, Role-based ID, Department, and Date of Joining.
- **Benefit:** Instant onboarding for new hires; professional office credentials.

### 2. Profile QR Codes
- **Description:** Unique QR codes generated for every employee record.
- **Components:** QR code appearing on the profile page and the Digital ID card.
- **Benefit:** Mobile-first verification; scan any card to instantly see the digital profile.

### 3. Automated Onboarding Emails
- **Description:** Automated email triggers upon account creation.
- **Components:** Send credentials (ID, Temporary Password) and dashboard links to the new hire.
- **Benefit:** Seamless, zero-effort hiring workflow.

### 4. Promotion & ID History Tracking
- **Description:** A historical timeline of role changes and ID transitions.
- **Benefit:** Audit trail for career growth (e.g., "Promoted from Employee to HR").

## 🛠️ Internal Refinement

### 5. Centralized Prefix Configuration
- **Description:** Move hardcoded prefixes (`MST_EMP`, etc.) to a central configuration file.
- **Benefit:** Easier re-branding if the company name changes.

### 6. Reactivation Archive
- **Description:** A toggle in the HR dashboard to view and reactivate "Soft Deleted" users.
- **Benefit:** Resuming data for boomerang employees (those who leave and return).
